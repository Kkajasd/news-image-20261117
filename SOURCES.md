## How the image was made

`tools/draw_frontpage.py` draws the whole front page with PIL:
masthead, headlines and decks set in Liberation Serif / DejaVu Sans,
plus small flat illustrations (the Kerch Bridge at dusk, a tanker,
the Manila–Shenzhen route, a market chart, a Vulcan rocket, the EU
stars). No photograph or generated image is used, so every word on
the page is legible and every story is the day's own.
